# TikTok SEO

Optimise every TikTok video and the profile behind it so they can be found in TikTok Search and chosen in the For You feed. This skill is the **TikTok SEO system only — TikTok, not YouTube Shorts**. YouTube Shorts has different limits (title 100 chars, tags field, 6 hashtags, clickable Related Video link) and is governed by the sibling skill `youtube-shorts-seo`; never apply a YouTube rule to a TikTok post, or a TikTok rule to a Short.

It does not replace your production skill: sourcing, permission, transformation, music hygiene, ban safety, strike response and visual generation live there. Where this skill and a YouTube post-writing skill disagree on a TikTok post, this skill governs TikTok, because it carries the TikTok evidence.

## Evidence labels — read before quoting anything

- **VERIFIED** — TikTok's own source, read live in full, URL + date given. Safe to quote as what TikTok says.
- **VERIFIED (search-indexed official text)** — official wording reproduced verbatim in search results AND corroborated by ≥2 independent secondary sources quoting the same wording. The page was not opened live.
- **REPORTED** — a named secondary source (study, creator guide, tracker). Quote it only with its source, date and caveats.
- **UNVERIFIED** — no source was found, or sources conflict. Never quote it as fact.
- **Recommendation / analysis** — a working rule derived from the evidence, not evidence itself.

TikTok publishes no search volumes, no ranking weights, no best time to post and no week-1–4 benchmark. Treat any exact ranking percentage, exact best-time hour, or exact week-1 target quoted anywhere as invented.

## Locked principles — these beat the research

- **One consistent hashtag line per channel.** Pick your channel's hashtag formula once (rule 18), log the choice and the date, and do not flip-flop between lines week to week. The choice of line is the channel owner's; this skill states the default and the alternative, never switches silently.
- **This skill applies to future videos only.** Do not retro-edit the caption or hashtags of an already-published TikTok to fit this skill. No evidence was found that a caption edit helps or hurts a TikTok video (UNVERIFIED); do not test it on a live video.
- **Name the game correctly, every time.** Never label one game's footage as another game's, and never use a game's hashtag, title or claim on another game's footage. Misleading metadata is a removal and strike risk.
- **The transformation rule beats every SEO rule in this file.** A raw re-upload of someone else's clip is ineligible for the For You feed under TikTok's own unoriginal-content rule (VERIFIED, §7 — see rule 43). No caption, hashtag, cover or posting time in this skill fixes an unoriginal clip. Every upload must carry the commentary / ranking / storyline layer your production skill requires, or it is posted knowing it will not reach the For You feed.

## 1. Profile — rules 1–10

1. **Username: 2–24 characters, letters/numbers/underscores/periods only, no trailing period.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Support "Changing your username", read live 19 Nov 2026; re-confirmed against independent limit guides checked Oct–Nov 2026.) Verify your handle resolves live at `https://www.tiktok.com/@[handle]` before changing anything else.
2. **Username changeable once every 30 days; changing it changes the profile link; a given-up handle is locked for an unpublished "short period".** (VERIFIED, same page.) Do not change the handle casually. Set it once, correctly, and leave it.
3. **Display name ("nickname"): up to 30 characters, changeable once every 7 days.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Support "Changing your nickname", read live 19 Nov 2026.) Recommended formula: `[Channel] | [Game/Topic] Clips` — 24 characters with short names (Template B). Whether the display name is indexed by TikTok search is UNVERIFIED (practitioner guides claim it; no official TikTok statement found) — use the keyword in the name anyway, it is low-risk and it tells a visitor what the account is. **Re-count after filling in your names** — the 24-character figure holds only for short names.
4. **Bio: 80 characters maximum.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Help Center "Changing account bio", checked Oct–Nov 2026 by multiple independent guides reading the same page.) Every letter, number, space and line break counts; emojis count (REPORTED). Use one of the three counted options in Template A (51–56 characters with short names, leaving margin for emoji counting differences). Some accounts now show a higher limit on the in-app counter, but TikTok has not documented a universal change — plan for 80, and use any extra room only if your own account's counter allows it. Never paste a long channel About text into the bio. Never paste the hashtag line into the bio — hashtags in bios are not clickable and waste budget (REPORTED).
5. **Plain-text URLs in the bio are NOT clickable.** (REPORTED.) Do not put a URL in the bio text. The clickable link lives in the separate Website/Links field (rule 6), not in the bio.
6. **No clickable bio link until 1,000 followers on a Personal account; a Business account gets one immediately.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Support "Accounts on TikTok", read live 19 Nov 2026; corroborated Nov 2026.) TikTok gives ONE website link, plus separately linkable Instagram and YouTube accounts from the same Edit-profile screen (REPORTED). At launch there is no clickable path off TikTok from the profile — do not promise a bio link in launch copy. The link is not available everywhere — check your own account. Whether the link affects SEO/discovery is UNVERIFIED.
7. **Account type: stay Personal unless you have a reason not to.** (Recommendation from VERIFIED facts.) TikTok's current model is Personal / Business / Organization — there is no separate "Creator" account any more (VERIFIED, Support "Accounts on TikTok", read live). Personal keeps BOTH the general music library and the Commercial Music Library, plus Creator Rewards eligibility; Business gets only the Commercial Music Library and cannot join Creator Rewards (VERIFIED, same page). Switching back loses business verification and it must be redone (VERIFIED). Business buys the day-one bio link at the price of the general music library — for a daily clips channel that trade is usually wrong. The account-type decision itself is the owner's; this rule states the recommendation and its cost.
8. **Profile photo: 200×200 px minimum, displayed as a circle; upload 400×400 px or larger.** (REPORTED.) Use a solid-background version of your logo (not a transparent one) — the circle crop cuts the four corners (~22% of image area), so keep the mark centred with nothing essential near the edges. The logo must read at ~30–40 px in comments: bold mark, high contrast, no fine text. A 6-second looping video avatar exists (REPORTED, ≥3 secondary sources) but was not verified against a readable official page, and whether it is available to all accounts/regions is UNVERIFIED — static photo first; test video avatar later only if the option appears in-app.
9. **No profile "category" picker exists to set.** (UNVERIFIED — no current official TikTok source lists a topic category picker, or says a category affects discovery.) Do not spend time looking for one. The only `GAMING`-style category found is a TikTok Business API advertising benchmark enum, not a profile/discovery signal (REPORTED).
10. **Verification (blue tick) is not a month-1 goal.** (REPORTED.) No follower minimum, but the binding criterion is notability — featured in multiple independent news sources, with sponsored/paid media excluded (all quoting TikTok's "Verified accounts on TikTok" help page). A new clips channel has no such coverage. Do not pay anyone for verification — TikTok says it never charges for it (VERIFIED, search-indexed official text).

## 2. Captions — rules 11–16

11. **Caption hard cap: 2,200 characters.** (VERIFIED — TikTok Content Posting API reference, corroborated by TikTok Newsroom Oct 2022 announcing the same 2,200 for the app, and by multiple independent limit guides checked Oct–Nov 2026.) Hashtags and @mentions count inside the 2,200. Keep every caption ≤2,200 characters — safe on every posting path (app, Studio, scheduler/API). **The 4,000-character figure is NOT a video-caption limit: it is the photo-post description limit in the same API reference.** Do not plan a video caption that depends on 4,000 working.
12. **Only ~100–150 characters show in the feed before "…more".** (REPORTED, multiple guides.) The exact cut varies by device/UI and is UNVERIFIED as a fixed number. **Hook + primary keyword phrase must sit in the first ~100 characters.** Everything after that is search-indexing text, not feed-visible text. Template C line 1 is 63–65 characters in the worked examples.
13. **One clear search phrase per video, early in the caption, repeated naturally in on-screen text.** (REPORTED, practitioner consensus.) TikTok itself does not publish a keyword-density or position rule (UNVERIFIED as official guidance). The phrase format is always [Creator/Character] + [Game] + [specific moment] — never the game alone.
14. **Do not stack irrelevant or misleading keywords because they are popular — TikTok says it will hurt traffic.** (REPORTED, via Creator Academy as quoted by multiple guides.) "A long list of hashtags will not make a video rank higher… overloading a caption with loosely related keywords or hashtags can work against you." One phrase per video; every word in the caption must describe this clip.
15. **Credit line on every video — in the caption, as plain text.** Credit the creator by handle with the original stream/video named (never a bare URL as the first line; TikTok links in captions are not the click path — the profile link is). Credit is good practice and an honesty signal, NOT a legal shield: TikTok's IP Policy lists the only defences as "proper authorization or legally valid reason" — credit is not listed (VERIFIED, §7, rule 44). Never write "no copyright intended" — it is not a defence and can read as an admission.
16. **Write a unique caption for every video.** (Recommendation, by analogy with YouTube's VERIFIED unique-description rule — no TikTok-specific unique-caption rule was found; the rule exists because identical captions across a batch are, on the face of TikTok's spam rules, "automation sending repetitive content" — REPORTED; no numeric threshold published.) Vary the hook sentence per clip; never paste the same caption across a batch. A short repeated sign-off line alone is lower-risk than a fully identical caption — that is inference, not a sourced rule.

## 3. Hashtags — rules 17–22

17. **Use 2–3 relevant hashtags per video; never more than 5.** (2–3: VERIFIED, search-indexed official text — Creator Academy "Elements of a TikTok video".) The 5-tag cap is now confirmed as a hard block, not a suggestion: since August 2025 the composer shows "Maximum 5 hashtags. Use accurate hashtags to get more views" and blocks a sixth tag (MediaPost 22 Aug 2025; Search Engine Land; composer-tested Sep 2026 by independent guides). TikTok has still published no official explanation for the cap. The operating rule is the same either way: never more than 5.
18. **The hashtag line (default formula): #[Game] #[GameVariant] #[Topic] #[Creator] #[Moment].** (Channel-owner decision; formula derived from the evidence.) Four base tags fit the 5-tag cap, leaving one slot for a moment tag. A moment hashtag (e.g. #[Game]Fails, #[Game]Glitch, #[Game]PoliceChase) may be added as the 5th when a moment keyword genuinely applies, never displacing the base tags. **The creator tag must be THE clip's creator** — never tag a creator who is not in the video. Never more than 5 total on TikTok (the YouTube skill's "never more than 6" does not apply here — TikTok's cap is 5).
19. **Hashtag order: #[Game] #[GameVariant] #[Creator], then #[Moment], then #[Topic] if used.** (REPORTED, observed winner pattern.) The game's main tag is the largest/fastest-growing tag in the niche; the variant tag appears in almost every observed hashtag block. Specific moment tags beat generic ones: #[Game]Glitch, #[Game]Fails, #[Game]FunnyMoments, #[Game]PoliceChase, #[Game]NPC.
20. **Never use #fyp / #foryou / #viral.** (REPORTED consensus — every 2026 guide agrees they carry no targeting signal; the underlying TikTok statement is UNVERIFIED, second-hand in every source.) They waste a slot (of only 5) that a relevant tag could occupy. A ranking penalty for them is not evidenced — the cost is opportunity cost, and under a 5-tag cap it is real.
21. **The TikTok-native alternative (owner's call, do not switch silently):** drop the #[Topic] tag (e.g. #GamingNews — a news/YouTube-style tag with no TikTok gaming-clip evidence found) and add #[Game]Clips instead, keeping the 5-tag shape: #[Game] #[GameVariant] #[Creator] #[Game]Clips #[Moment]. The evidence for #[Game]Clips is directional only (a cached dataset showing higher average views per post — averages skewed by viral posts, UNVERIFIED as a causal claim). The default line (rule 18) stays until the owner chooses otherwise. Log the choice and the date; do not flip-flop between lines week to week.
22. **Hashtags are a categorisation / topic signal, not a reach driver.** (REPORTED, convergent 2026 guides.) TikTok's Newsroom lists hashtags as one component of "video information" in For You ranking (VERIFIED, read live 23 Nov 2026) — not a standalone driver, and TikTok gives them no weight. Hashtag-page traffic volume is UNVERIFIED. Do not cite any "% impression boost from hashtags" figure — the only 2026 numbers found are correlational (videos with any hashtags got ~3.7% more views than their account's median in one 1,569-video study; the study itself says that does not show tags cause more views — REPORTED).

## 4. Search + For You — rules 23–27

23. **Search values relevance over views and engagement.** (VERIFIED, search-indexed official text — Creator Academy, "Driving video traffic: Search".) A zero-follower account can rank for a specific long-tail query when relevance beats the incumbents. Relevance is determined by "video captions, video text, and hashtags" (VERIFIED, search-indexed official text — Creator Academy, "Search: The Basics"). Put the phrase in all three: caption line 1, opening on-screen text, hashtags.
24. **Completion is the strongest For You signal TikTok names.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Newsroom, read live 23 Nov 2026.) "A strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator." Cut dead air, open on the payoff, end on the payoff. A 52s clip watched to the end beats a 3-minute clip abandoned at 5 seconds. No completion threshold is published — any "70% needed to go viral" figure, or any completion-rate-by-length table, is UNVERIFIED. Do not adopt one from a guide that prints it without a TikTok source.
25. **Follower count and past hits are NOT direct ranking factors.** (VERIFIED, TikTok Newsroom, read live 23 Nov 2026.) "Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system." Do not buy followers to "unlock" distribution — bought followers buy no distribution, and buying them is prohibited (rule 47).
26. **Sound is a matching signal, not a boost.** (VERIFIED that sound is a named ranking input — Newsroom lists "sounds" under video information; Help Center lists "the sound used" under content information for search.) TikTok has NOT published that using a trending sound gets a video more reach (UNVERIFIED). Do not replace the clip's original reaction audio with trending music to chase a boost that is unevidenced (see rule 30).
27. **No invented numbers.** (Rule.) TikTok publishes no ranking weights, no retention threshold, no test-batch size, no best time, no week-1 benchmark. Never quote "watch time is X%", "200–500 test viewers", "post at 7pm", or any week-1 views/followers target as a TikTok fact. All such figures in secondary sources are guesses or guru lore (UNVERIFIED).

## 5. Covers, sounds, music — rules 28–31

28. **Set the cover BEFORE posting; you cannot reliably change it after.** (REPORTED — TikTok's help pages "don't describe a way" to change a cover after posting; one source claims a short edit window exists, another says delete-and-repost is the only sure way — UNVERIFIED.) In-app: record/upload → Next → tap **Select cover** → drag to the frame → Save, then post. If nothing is set, the cover defaults to the first frame (0 ms). If scheduling via a tool, ask whether it sends a custom image or only a frame — if only a frame, put the cover text *inside the video* on the frame you will pick (REPORTED, API nuance). The cover is the video's listing image in the 2-column search-results grid, where viewers choose on cover + view count alone (REPORTED) — its evidenced effect is click-through in search/profile, not ranking. Design: 2–3 words max, upper-middle safe zone (Y 350–1100px on a 1080×1920 frame), high-contrast brand colours, short moment label ("FIRST GUNFIGHT", "HE LOST IT"), out of the UI zones (top ~220px, bottom caption/sound-disc zone, right interaction icons). Profile grid crops to 3:4 vertical (1080×1440 centre crop) — keep the key subject/text in the centre.
29. **Burn the keyword into on-screen text in the opening seconds.** (VERIFIED that Creator Academy names "video text" as a search-relevance input; REPORTED practitioner consensus that TikTok reads overlays via OCR.) Do NOT rely on spoken words alone — whether the ASR transcript feeds the search index is UNVERIFIED, and fast, overlapping speech is unreliable. Enable auto-captions anyway (accessibility + a possible third signal), but they are not the plan. (Recommendation: keep on-screen text to ≤3 layers, large and bold, each visible ≥0.5s, keyword visible in the first 2 seconds, out of the bottom third — decorative/hand-written fonts are missed by OCR, REPORTED.)
30. **Original stream audio is the default sound.** (Recommendation from the evidence.) The clips' value *is* the live reaction audio. Replacing it with trending music removes the searchable spoken layer and the reason to watch, for a discovery boost that is unevidenced (rule 26). Trending music is an optional overlay only if it does not bury the reaction.
31. **No licensed music audible that has not been cleared in the edit.** (VERIFIED that TikTok's IP Policy covers music as copyrighted work; REPORTED that TikTok can mute or remove a video after posting, not only at upload.) In-game radio / licensed songs audible in the stream can get a video muted, removed or struck; there is no "incidental background music" exception in TikTok's published policy and no published mute-vs-remove threshold (UNVERIFIED). A clip that is mostly the creator's voice + game SFX is lower-risk than one with a recognisable song. If the account ever becomes Business, trending sounds disappear the same day (only the ~1M-track Commercial Music Library — REPORTED); on a Personal account the general library is licensed for personal, non-commercial use only (REPORTED).

## 6. Pins, playlists, Series — rules 32–34

32. **Pin up to 3 videos; pinning does not boost reach.** (REPORTED — 3 max, consistent across sources; "It does not change how the video performs in the For You feed or affect its distribution… Pinned videos do not receive special algorithm treatment.") Pinning is profile conversion only: the first 3 videos a new visitor sees. Only your own posted videos can be pinned; Stories, LIVE replays, drafts and scheduled posts cannot; a deleted/private video loses its pin automatically. Pick the first 3 by hook strength at launch (Template E); rotate to the top 3 by average watch time once 10 posts exist. Do not expect pinning to revive a dead video.
33. **Playlists and Series both need 10,000 followers — unavailable at launch.** (REPORTED — Creator Academy via multiple Oct–Nov 2026 guides, re-checked Nov 2026.) Playlists: free grouping, name ≤25 chars, each video in only one Playlist. Series: paywalled collection, up to 80 videos of up to 20 minutes each, $0.99–$189.99, price locked once posted, **original content required** — a Series built from third-party clips is the opposite of what the rule asks for; Series videos do not earn Creator Rewards. Series eligibility adds: account at least 30 days old, 3+ public videos in the last 30 days, 1,000+ views in the last 30 days, age 18+. Do not plan either before 10k. When Playlists unlock, build themed sets for bingeing and profile conversion — their discovery value is indirect (profile conversion + session watch time), not a FYP ranking boost (REPORTED as indirect; a direct ranking effect is UNVERIFIED).
34. **First comment: one specific question tied to THIS clip.** (Recommendation, by analogy with the YouTube pinned-comment rules — no TikTok-specific pinned-comment study was found.) Post it from the channel account in the first hour; end in a question to seed replies. How many comments can be pinned per video is UNVERIFIED (sources conflict: 1 vs 3) — check in-app before relying on it. Never a bare URL or a link-first line in the comment; never the same text on two videos. Note: the YouTube first-comment rule of publish +60 minutes does NOT transfer here — no TikTok evidence supports it; the first hour is the recommendation.

## 7. Cadence + posting — rules 35–38

35. **Post 1/day; 2/day maximum, spaced hours apart, only if hook quality holds.** (Recommendation from REPORTED evidence.) TikTok sets no fixed posting number (REPORTED — Creator Academy: "there's no one-size-fits-all answer"; "your exact cadence is up to you"). The famous "1–4/day" is 2021 advertiser testing advice, not a creator rule (REPORTED). The largest dataset (Buffer, Oct 2025, 11.4M posts, ~150k accounts) shows the efficient step is 1/week → 2–5/week (+17% views/post); median views/post barely moves (489 → 506 → 487 → 459) — more posts raise the ceiling (90th percentile 3,722 → 14,401), not the typical video. The evidence does not support 3+/day as a requirement for a new account.
36. **There is no universal best time — test your own.** (REPORTED.) TikTok publishes no best time. The big studies contradict each other directly: Sprout (Mar 2026, ~2B engagements) says Tue–Thu 2–6 pm, Sunday worst; Buffer (Sep 2026, 7.1M posts) says Saturday best, Sunday 9 am best slot; Revid (Oct 2026, 176,367 TikToks) says 3–6 am ET best, evenings worst. Generic tables are not usable for a US/international gaming audience. Test 2–3 windows across the first 10 posts (one US-evening ET, one US-morning ET, one weekend slot), hold format constant, judge after 10+ posts. From 100 followers, set windows from TikTok Studio's follower-activity chart — the only audience-specific source.
37. **Week 1: no burst, no mass following, no automation, one consistent IP.** (REPORTED operator experience — TikTok publishes no numeric limits; one reader of all 218 Help Center articles, Oct 2026, found none that sets a number.) Creator-reported ranges: brand-new accounts get follow-blocked around 20–140 follows/day; posting a burst on day 1 is listed as a reduced-distribution trigger; automation in week 1 is "almost certainly flagged". Post manually for at least the first 30 days. Complete the profile, verify email/phone, watch and engage normally for 1–2 days before the first post. Follow slowly and only accounts in the niche.
38. **Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, MP4/MOV, H.264.** (REPORTED — TikTok Ads spec + developer docs.) Length limits depend on how you post — the old "3 minutes in-app" figure is wrong (VERIFIED, TikTok Help Center "Camera tools", read Oct 2026 by multiple independent guides): **record in the app up to 10 minutes; upload in the app up to 60 minutes; TikTok Studio on the web up to 30 minutes; through the API / a scheduling tool up to 10 minutes; 4 GB API file cap.** The 60-minute upload is not on every account — check your own upload screen. Length is set by the moment (cut dead air, end on the payoff) — no TikTok source states a particular duration ranks better in search (UNVERIFIED). Note the trade-off: a clip under 1 minute is under the Creator Rewards floor (rule 46).

## 8. Analytics + testing — rules 39–42

39. **Analytics are available from the first public post — no follower minimum for basic metrics.** (REPORTED.) TikTok Studio (app / tiktok.com/tiktokstudio) is the home; Business accounts see Business Suite. Follower demographics need **100 followers**; Creator Search Insights "Searches by followers" needs **1,000 followers** (REPORTED, multiple sources agree on both). Figures can take 1–2 days to update.
40. **Measure 6 numbers per video, every 7 days: views, avg watch time as % of length, full-watch %, saves, shares, traffic-source split (For You vs Search vs Profile).** (REPORTED, guide consensus.) Judge on the **last 10 posts as a set** against their own median, never on one video and never against other accounts. Search share rising = SEO working. Saves + shares + comments are stronger intent than likes. Profile views + net follower change per post show which topics convert viewers to followers.
41. **There is no week-1–4 benchmark — do not invent one.** (UNVERIFIED — no published benchmark for a brand-new account's first 1–4 weeks was found.) Platform context only, never a pass/fail line: Socialinsider engagement by views 3.85% (H1 2026); Buffer median views/post ~489–506. Any "week-1 views" or "1,000 followers in 2–4 weeks" target quoted to the owner would be fabricated.
42. **Use Creator Search Insights weekly for topics, not for exact queries.** (REPORTED.) Free in-app tool (Mar 2024 launch): Content gap = topics "searched for often, but aren't featured in a large number of videos" — high demand, low supply. Search Analytics shows search views, search view %, search impressions, average CTR, average search ranking for your posts. **There is no verified TikTok Studio report listing the exact search queries that found this account** — that is performance *in search*, not a query log. Some topics are AI-generated and TikTok "cannot guarantee the accuracy" of them. The tool is available in select regions only — if your account does not have it, that is a rollout gap, not a setting you missed.

## 9. Rules that bind — rules 43–48

43. **Do not build the channel on raw re-uploads — they are For You-ineligible by TikTok's own definition.** (VERIFIED — Community Guidelines, effective 24 Sep 2026: content is ineligible for the For You feed "if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new"; Creator Rewards definition, VERIFIED (read live): "videos that are sped up or contain filters, fixed texts, or stickers" do NOT count as creative edits.) An ineligible video stays up but "won't be displayed in the For You feed" (VERIFIED, search-indexed official text — FYF Eligibility Standards); accounts that repeatedly post FYF-ineligible content "may be made ineligible for the FYF and harder to find" (REPORTED). The mitigation is the production skill's transformation: original voiceover commentary, ranking, analysis or storyline throughout — not a speed change, filter, fixed text or sticker. **This rule outranks every other rule in this file.**
44. **Crediting the creator does NOT protect the account.** (VERIFIED — TikTok IP Policy, read live: defences are "proper authorization or legally valid reason" only; credit is not listed. REPORTED — multiple 2025–2026 guides, unanimous.) A stream clip is two copyrights at once: the streamer's performance/broadcast and the game footage (publisher). Either rightsholder can file a TikTok copyright report. TikTok does not adjudicate fair-use disputes between third parties (VERIFIED, IP Policy, read live). Infringing content is REMOVED; repeat infringement gets the account BANNED under TikTok's repeat-infringer policy (VERIFIED, IP Policy, read live). Strikes expire after 90 days (VERIFIED, search-indexed official text). "Three copyright strikes = ban" is REPORTED (single source) — do not quote it as confirmed. Before building on any game's footage, read that publisher's fan-content / video policy: most tolerate non-commercial use and reserve takedown at any time — monetising the footage is tolerated, not permitted.
45. **No casino/gambling clips, no spoilers/leaks/isolated cutscenes/cheat-exploit clips.** (REPORTED — reading the Guidelines effective 24 Sep 2026: showing/promoting gambling is kept off the For You feed and age-restricted 18+; on LIVE, participating in gambling is not allowed in any region. VERIFIED (search-indexed official text) — Rockstar's fan-content policy, last updated 10 Jan 2025, as the worked example: Take-Two "reserves the right to have its copyrighted material taken down at any time"; takedown examples include pre-release/leaked footage, the ending or big story reveals, isolated cutscenes and in-game TV posted on their own, and videos promoting cheats/glitches/money exploits.) A game with an in-game casino makes gambling clips FYF-ineligible at best. The general rule: a publisher's takedown list beats any SEO plan — check it before the clip, not after the strike.
46. **Check whether Creator Rewards runs in YOUR country before planning revenue around it.** (VERIFIED — Creator Rewards Program, read live: Personal account only; ≥10,000 followers; ≥100,000 views in last 30 days; videos ≥1 minute; original content "designed, filmed, and produced by you"; each video needs 1,000 qualified For You views before it earns. Country list, confirmed Nov 2026 via Creator Academy as quoted by multiple guides: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil — 8 countries. Claims of further countries (Canada, Italy, Spain, Australia) appear in single sources and are UNVERIFIED — do not plan on them.) A clips channel fails the test three ways at once: sub-1-minute clips fail the 1-minute floor, reused clips fail originality, and many countries fail regardless. LIVE Gifts (1,000 followers) is the only other TikTok-native route, and its payout availability by country is UNVERIFIED. Any monetisation plan must rely on off-TikTok routes — and using game footage "as part of a promotion for a product or service" is outside most publishers' non-commercial tolerance (rule 45).
47. **Never buy followers, join pods, or use automation to inflate engagement.** (VERIFIED — Integrity and Authenticity, read live: "We do not allow the trade or marketing of services that attempt to artificially increase engagement or deceive TikTok's recommendation system. If we become aware of accounts or content with inauthentically inflated metrics, we will remove the associated fake followers or likes."; "Content is ineligible for the FYF if it seeks to trick or manipulate others to increase gifts, followers, likes, views, or other engagement metrics.") Penalties range from FYP/search exclusion to restriction to ban (REPORTED). Follower count is not a direct FYP factor anyway (rule 25).

## 10. Keyword tiers — the method

Sort candidate phrases into three tiers before you commit to a calendar. Verify every term before you publish by searching it in TikTok (signed out where possible) and inspecting the top results. TikTok publishes no search volumes, so no numeric volumes appear in this skill. Run the top Tier 1 terms through Creator Search Insights (Content gap) before locking the month-1 calendar. Appendix A shows the method applied to one game — the tiers are the method; the lists are one dated example.

- **Tier 1 — attack first:** proven demand, winnable competition (small channels hold top spots, exact-match titles are thin). One specific moment per video: one glitch (exact glitch name in caption + on-screen text), one fail, one character + specific verb/moment — never the bare term alone. The moment noun does the work; the bucket word ("funny moments", "fails") does not rank on its own.
- **Tier 2 — build toward:** demand proven, competition harder or format mismatch (compilation-sized topics, guide terms, head terms). Use as second keyword, not the lead caption phrase. The head term (game name alone) is a prefix + hashtag, never a bare caption.
- **Tier 3 — do not lead with:** saturated, dead-end, expired, or wrong-format terms. Never a Tier 3 term as the lead. Includes: any game term on another game's footage; single words such as funny, viral or trending as a caption; news-owned terms that die within 48h (reviews, "is it out", download size); leaks (a ban risk); a mode the game does not have at launch.
- **Deleted from Tier 1 — do not lead with these:** terms with no evidence for this game — they are usually substitutions from another game in the same franchise. A term survives as a compilation label, not as a lead term, until 3+ genuine clips exist for it.
- **Spelling rule:** caption line 1 = "[Creator] [Game] [moment]" or "[Game] [moment]"; on-screen text = "[Game] [moment]"; hashtags = the rule-18 line. Use the official game spelling in the visible caption line 1; the search spelling (if different) is carried in hashtags and second keywords. Never a spaceless game name in the visible caption line 1.
- **One head term, one video (keyword cannibalisation).** (Recommendation.) Posting five videos all targeting the same head term splits the algorithm's view of them — target the head term once, target long-tail variants for the others.
- **Never re-upload streamer clips without the transformation layer.** Own gameplay + commentary only, long-term. Name a streamer only in commentary about a moment, never as the clip source without the layer.
