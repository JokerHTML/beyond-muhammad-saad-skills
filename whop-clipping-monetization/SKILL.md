# Whop Clipping Monetization

Turn game clipping into real money on Whop, in the right order, without spending money you don't have, and without selling anything that gets the account, the channel, or the money taken away. Two lanes: **earn as a clipper** (join funded Content Rewards campaigns, get paid per 1,000 verified views) and **earn as a seller** (sell a workflow-only starter product on your Whop, never the footage).

## Evidence labels — read before quoting anything

- **FACT** — Whop's own docs, Content Rewards' own help pages, or Rockstar's own policy page, re-verified live 23 Nov 2026 (or dated below).
- **REPORTED** — a named secondary source, with date and caveats. Best available evidence, not the platform's own word.
- **UNVERIFIED** — no source found, or two sources that contradict each other. Never quote as fact, never build a price or a promise on it.
- **Recommendation** — a working rule derived from the evidence, not evidence itself.

Nothing in this skill invents a number. If a figure would strengthen a line and you do not have a verified one, write the line without it.

- **Never quote a budget-left, rate, cap, or clipper count from memory.** Read it off the campaign page in the same session.
- **Fees and platform rules change too.** The headline checkout rates below were re-verified on Whop's own fee page on 23 Nov 2026; re-check `docs.whop.com/fees` before quoting them to a buyer.

Monetized game clipping is **tolerated, not licensed** — Take-Two can take material down at any time. That is why this skill's first rule is: sell the workflow, never the footage, and earn clipper pay only under a campaign brief that authorizes the footage. ## 5. Earning as a clipper (Content Rewards) + affiliates + recruiting

### 5.1 How Content Rewards works

Content Rewards (`contentrewards.com`) is a campaign marketplace run by its own company, Content Rewards Inc. — **not** part of Whop. Whop is the payment processor: sign-in, money movement, identity checks (FACT — Content Rewards' own About page and Creator Terms, Nov 2026). A brand, streamer, or agency funds a budget and pays clippers per 1,000 verified views on clips posted on the clipper's own TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels / X accounts.

- **Campaign types:** CPM (pay per 1,000 views, with a minimum and a cap per clip), per-post (flat amount per approved clip), retainer (fixed sum per cycle), and **pool** (a fixed pot split between approved clippers by their share of counted views — no per-clip rate at all). Read which type you are joining: pool math is nothing like CPM math.
- **Campaign minimums (FACT — Content Rewards' own pages, Nov 2026):** campaigns start at a **$1,000** budget. Brands pay a 10% platform fee on each creator payout (8% if the organization is verified), plus a processing fee shown at funding.
- **Joining is free. You must be 18+** — the Creator Terms bar anyone under 18, even with parental consent (REPORTED — Creator Terms, via guides Nov 2026).

### 5.2 Joining campaigns — the fastest first dollar

1. **Sign up at contentrewards.com.** Whop handles sign-in, money, and identity checks.
2. **Connect the accounts you post from** — TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X. Instagram must be a Professional/Business/Creator account. An account linked only in Whop's settings does NOT count — link it in Content Rewards.
3. **Pick from the live list the day you join** — budgets drop as clips get paid. Read §5.3 for the method and the dated worked example. **Never join from a table in a skill or a guide.**
4. **Read the brief before every clip.** The brief is the permission slip and the rulebook: which streams to clip, where to post, what the caption must say, which tags/disclosures (#ad at the start if asked). FTC disclosure is on you.
5. **Post, then submit the link within 30 minutes.** A post older than that cannot be submitted; a post can only ever be submitted once (REPORTED — Content Rewards FAQ, via guides Nov 2026; confirmed in agency briefs — see §5.4).
6. **Expect review:** automatic Bot Score fraud check first, then a human at the brand approves or rejects. Rejected = $0. Never buy views — fraud checks catch it and the account is the thing at risk.
7. **Payout math (FACT — Content Rewards help center, Nov 2026):** CPM rounds of 7 days earning + 3-day hold (≈10 days to settle); recurring CPM pay ends 30 days after the clip first meets its minimum; per-post pays ~15 minutes after approval once the clip hits its minimum; **10% creator fee** off the top on current-version CPM campaigns (per-post/retainer: 10% under a $5,000 budget, **0% at $5,000 or more**; older campaigns can still show 7%); minimum withdrawal **$1** from Content Rewards Balance to Whop Balance (free move); Whop withdrawal to bank/PayPal/wallet ≈ up to 5 business days standard, instant costs a Whop fee.
8. **Track every campaign in a notes file:** rate, minimum, cap, platforms, rules, date joined, clips submitted, views, earnings. A campaign's budget can be mostly paid out already — check before investing edit time.

**Payouts to your country — check before you count on it.** Whop's own payout docs list 200+ supported countries and name Pakistan in the supported-countries list; minimum withdrawal threshold $10 (FACT — Whop Docs "Set Up Payouts", read live 21 Nov 2026; corroborated by TechJuice, Mar 2026, with a direct Whop statement). The exact payout fee for a local bank transfer varies by country and shows only in Dashboard > Balances — UNVERIFIED until you open that screen. Payout speed to your country and any local-currency minimum are likewise UNVERIFIED until the setup flow is attempted. PayPal payout is offered by Whop generally, but PayPal does not operate in Pakistan, so it is not a usable route there.

### 5.3 How to pick a campaign (the method — this is the durable part)

On the campaign page, read these six numbers before you clip anything:

| # | Field | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Type** | CPM, per-post, retainer, or pool? Pool = split pot, no per-clip rate. |
| 2 | **Rate** | $ per 1,000 views (CPM only). Median across live campaigns ≈ $1.00 (Nov 2026 checks: median $1.0875 on 10 Nov; $1.00 on 18 Nov; range $0.25–$3.00). |
| 3 | **Minimum** | What a clip must earn before it is reviewed/paid (typical ≈ $2 ≈ 2,000 views at $1). Below it = $0. |
| 4 | **Cap** | Maximum one clip can earn (typical ≈ $200). Above it, views stop paying. |
| 5 | **Budget left** | What is still fundable. A campaign at 95% spent is nearly closed. |
| 6 | **Brief rules** | Which exact footage, which platforms, required tags/captions, audience rules, deadlines. **This is the permission slip — see §5.4.** |

**Priority order (Recommendation):** highest rate with a cap you can realistically reach, on footage you are authorized to clip, with budget left above ~20%. Avoid the lowest-rate campaigns ($0.30–$0.35/1K) unless the clip is already made and costs nothing extra to post. In a pool, divide the pot by the approved-clipper count before you decide — a $10,000 pool split among 2,500 clippers pays very differently from the same pool split among 500.

## 6. Rights & safety gates (run before ANYTHING is sold or posted for pay)

**Gate 1 — Rockstar / Take-Two (game footage).** The fan policy covers **non-commercial** use only. "Non-commercial means you don't make money through the game footage you post or use the material as part of a promotion for a product or service." Take-Two reserves the right to have its copyrighted material taken down at any time. Licensing questions: copyright@take2games.com. (FACT — Rockstar Games Customer Support, "Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material", updated 10 Jan 2025, re-verified 23 Nov 2026.)
- Consequence: **never sell footage.** Sell the workflow. The product in §2 contains zero game footage.
- **Endings/isolated cutscenes:** Rockstar names them for takedown. Never sell them, never post them as standalone products, and keep any compilation commentary-led (narration + story structure + short segments + cleared creators).
- **Using game content for generative AI is banned** by Rockstar's ToS (REPORTED — FanRules, checked 6 Nov 2026). Do not sell AI-generated game visuals as a product.
- Monetized game content is tolerated, not licensed. Any income from it rests on the rights holder continuing to allow it. If money is the plan, treat the footage as licensed material and ask.

**Gate 2 — The streamer.** A streamer's clip/share button licenses sharing **only as necessary to enable that functionality** — it does not cover downloading and reposting for pay. Credit is not permission. The permission that counts: (a) the streamer's own campaign brief, (b) a published clipping program, (c) written permission from the streamer. (FACT — Twitch ToS, last modified 12 Aug 2026, as read Nov 2026; REPORTED — LuminaClippers/KeyMo.)
- **Build your own clearance list and keep it written down.** For each streamer, record which of (a)/(b)/(c) applies, with the date and the source link. Never clip on "they probably don't mind."
- Dated example from Nov 2026 research (re-verify before relying on it): ohnePixel ran a public Discord clippers program; QTCinderella had DMCA-threat history and was a never-clip; several major streamers required written permission. These were true for one channel's research in Nov 2026 — they are not standing facts about those streamers.

**Gate 3 — YouTube.** Reused content: compilations "with little or no narrative" are flagged **even with permission**; a YPP rejection hits the whole channel. The 1 Oct 2026 originality update cuts reach of re-uploads "without adding anything of your own". In-game/radio music triggers Content ID. Every clip posted for pay must carry your own transformation (hook, captions, layout, commentary) — not a raw re-upload. (FACT — YouTube's reused-content policy and 1 Oct 2026 originality update.)

**Gate 4 — Whop.** IP-infringing products are a prohibited business. Fake followers/likes/engagement are prohibited. Fake reviews are prohibited (Whop verifies reviews internally). False/deceptive earnings claims are prohibited conduct. "Lifetime access" offers are banned. Dispute rate must stay **below 1%** rolling; the seller carries every refund/chargeback; suspended balances are held 90 days by default. Dispute fee is **contested ($15 vs $155)** — check the live seller terms before quoting it. (FACT — Whop Docs, Prohibited Businesses / Community Guidelines, read Nov 2026; REPORTED — DoesItPay, Oct 2026.)

**Gate 5 — The buyer.** The listing must say what is inside, who it's for, and what it is not (§2.2). Vague listings get clicks and refunds, not members who stay — and churn is the #1 Discover rank killer.

## 7. DO-NOT-DO list

1. Never sell clip packs of other streamers' game footage.
2. Never promise views, earnings, or income — in a listing, a post, a DM, or a thumbnail.
3. Never use a fake-earnings screenshot, a bought review, or a fabricated testimonial.
4. Never sell "lifetime access".
5. Never clip a streamer without a brief, a published program, or written permission.
6. Never use leaked/pre-release footage, endings, or isolated cutscenes as standalone products.
7. Never use game content for generative AI products (Rockstar's ToS bans it for GTA; check your game's terms for the same).
8. Never buy views, followers, or engagement for a campaign clip (Bot Score + fraud checks; the account is at risk).
9. Never quote a local-currency payout amount before a real payout lands.
10. Never run a $1,000 campaign, buy a paid tool, or fund a retainer until product/campaign revenue exists.
11. Never put another creator's name in a listing as if they endorse the product. (Streamer names appear only as "clips from X's streams" in factual descriptions, with credit — never as endorsement.)
12. Never copy another seller's assets, copy, or course content. Build yours from your own work.
13. Never join a campaign from a table in a skill, a guide, or a screenshot — read the live campaign page the same day.

## 8. Metrics + weekly review (every Monday, 10 minutes)

Track in one sheet (create it at launch — `[your-workspace]/whop-tracker.md`):
- Content Rewards: campaigns joined, clips submitted, clips approved, views, gross earnings, 10% fee, net in Whop balance.
- Whop store: listing impressions, clicks, page views, purchases, refund/dispute count, member count (free tier), retention (week-1 churn), reviews (count + stars).
- Funnel: Shorts views → Whop profile visits → purchases. If impressions are low, the fix is listing SEO (title/description keywords, category, completeness) + external traffic — not a price cut.
- **Kill/keep rules:** if the $9 product has 0 sales after 30 days of real traffic, the problem is the listing or the traffic, not the price — fix copy and distribution before discounting. If week-1 churn is above ~40%, the listing over-promises — rewrite it to match what the product actually delivers. If a campaign rejects 2+ clips in a row, stop and re-read its brief before posting another.
